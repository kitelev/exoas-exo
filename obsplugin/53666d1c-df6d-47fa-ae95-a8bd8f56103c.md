# $obsplugin

Ontology for **other-plugin** settings (e.g. Periodic Notes) distributed via the homoiconic settings framework (RFC `f402002b`). Home of `obsplugin__Setting` / `obsplugin__SettingKey` (subclasses of `setting__Setting` / `setting__SettingKey`). Populated with instances in M4.
