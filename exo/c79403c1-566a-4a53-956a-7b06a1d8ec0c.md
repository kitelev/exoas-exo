Architectural layer an `exo__Ontology` belongs to — a closed-world enumeration ordering ontologies
from foundation upward. Sealed set of four members; new layers require a TBox change, not runtime data.

| member | `_order` | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `exo__OntologyLayerFoundation` | 0 | platform core — everything else may depend on it, it depends on nothing |
| `exo__OntologyLayerDomain` | 10 | domain vocabularies (efforts, concepts, people, periods) |
| `exo__OntologyLayerApplication` | 20 | application-level composition (commands, UI, profiles) |
| `exo__OntologyLayerDocumentation` | 30 | documentation and requirement layers, depended on by nobody |

`exo__OntologyLayer_order` is the machine-readable rank: a dependency edge is layer-conformant when it
points from a HIGHER `_order` to a LOWER or equal one. The numbering leaves gaps of 10 so an
intermediate layer can be inserted without renumbering existing members.

Subclass of `exo__Enum` (closed-world, sealed, sibling-disjoint) — the second such subclass alongside
`exocmd__SubstitutionTokenLegacy`.
