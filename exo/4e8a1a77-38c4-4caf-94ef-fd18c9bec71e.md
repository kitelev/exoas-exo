The architectural layer this ontology belongs to. Range `exo__OntologyLayer`, cardinality 0..1 —
an ontology declares at most one layer; an absent value means the layer is not yet classified
(open-world, no default is assumed).

Read together with `exo__OntologyLayer_order` it makes the dependency direction checkable: an
`exo__Ontology_imports` / `exo__AssetSpace_dependsOn` edge is layer-conformant when it runs from a
higher `_order` to a lower or equal one. A violation means a foundation ontology has come to depend
on an application one — the cycle-prone direction.
