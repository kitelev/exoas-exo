The allow-list of `exo__Class` values whose instances may be co-located under this
ontology. Multi-value. Consumed by `exocortex audit ontology-membership` (the
cohesion-side KSD ArchUnit fitness-function, req c23f6f50): a member is admitted
iff its `exo__Instance_class` — or a `exo__Class_superClass` ancestor
(subsumption, e.g. `exo__Property` covers `exo__ObjectProperty`/`exo__DatatypeProperty`)
— is in this list. Include `exo__Ontology` so the ontology's own self-anchor is a
legitimate member. An ontology WITHOUT `exo__Ontology_admits` is fail-open (its
members are not membership-checked — audit-first).
