Rank of an `exo__OntologyLayer` member within the layering. Domain `exo__OntologyLayer`,
range `xsd:integer`, cardinality 0..1.

Lower is more foundational: 0 = Foundation, 10 = Domain, 20 = Application, 30 = Documentation.
Gaps of 10 leave room to insert an intermediate layer without renumbering existing members.

The property exists so the ordering lives in the graph rather than in a hardcoded list inside the
engine: a layer-conformance check compares two `_order` values and needs no knowledge of the member
names (homoiconicity invariant — user-configurable semantics as RDF data).
